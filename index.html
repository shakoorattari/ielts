IELTS Band Builder: Free Study Guide with 1000 Collocations

A free IELTS study guide built around the 1000 collocations that help candidates write and speak naturally, with flashcards that adapt to what you remember and model essays to learn from. There is no account to create, and you can add it to your phone’s home screen to use it like an app, even without a connection.

What you can practise

  • 1000 collocations in 10 themes and 100 topics, each with a meaning and an example sentence, searchable by topic or phrase.
  • Flashcards with spaced repetition: cards you find hard come back sooner.
  • Fill the blank drills that train you to recall the missing phrase.
  • Quick quizzes that test the meanings of collocations.
  • Writing practice: write your own sentences for each topic and tick off the phrases you used.
  • 50 synonym upgrades for the plain words that appear in most essays (people, important, good…), with flashcards, quizzes, typed recall, rewrite drills, an essay vocabulary scanner and a timed writing test.
  • An optional AI coach that gives feedback on your own writing. In Google Chrome on a computer (and Microsoft Edge's developer preview) it runs on your own device, so your text is not sent anywhere. On other devices you can use your own free AI key, and then your text goes to the provider you chose, only when you press an AI button.
  • Model essays for IELTS Writing Task 2 with the key phrases highlighted and explained, and an A–Z phrase bank.
  • Progress tracking, a daily streak and your attempt history.
  • Install it as an app on Android, iPhone, iPad or a computer. Once installed, everything except the optional AI coach and sync works offline.

Your progress stays with you

Everything is saved in your own browser. There are no accounts and no advertising or analytics. You can optionally sync your progress between devices through a private GitHub Gist that you control. Two explainer videos are embedded, but nothing is requested from YouTube unless you press play.
