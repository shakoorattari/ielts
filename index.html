IELTS Band Builder: Free Study Guide with 1000 Collocations

A free IELTS study guide built around the 1000 collocations that help candidates write and speak naturally, with flashcards that adapt to what you remember and model essays to learn from. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

What you can practise

  • 1000 collocations in 10 themes and 100 topics, each with a meaning and an example sentence, searchable by topic or phrase.
  • Flashcards with spaced repetition: cards you find hard come back sooner.
  • Fill the blank drills that train you to recall the missing phrase.
  • Quick quizzes that test the meanings of collocations.
  • Writing practice: write your own sentences for each topic and tick off the phrases you used.
  • Model essays for IELTS Writing Task 2 with the key phrases highlighted and explained, and an A–Z phrase bank.
  • Progress tracking, a daily streak and your attempt history.

Your progress stays with you

Everything is saved in your own browser. There are no accounts and no advertising or analytics. You can optionally sync your progress between devices through a private GitHub Gist that you control.
